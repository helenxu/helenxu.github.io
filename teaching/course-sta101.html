STA101 魅力统计 Fascinating Statistics

3 学分 · 每周 3 学时 · 专业导论课 · Instructor: Cong Xu

课程简介

This course introduces the basic knowledge in statistics and data science through classic historical stories, hot events on the Internet, and real industry cases, etc. By taking the course, students are expected to have a preliminary understanding of the basic knowledge in statistics and data science, such as types of data, random variables, probability distributions, parameter estimation, hypothesis testing, etc., know the background of statistical methods and that statistics and data science are problem-driven disciplines.

本课程通过历史经典故事、网络热点事件、行业真实案例等引出对统计学与数据科学知识点的介绍。 通过本课程学习，学生预期能够对统计学与数据科学中的基础知识有初步的了解，如数据类型、 随机变量、概率分布、参数估计、假设检验等，对一些统计方法产生的背景和历史有一定认知， 并由此知道统计学与数据科学是问题驱动的学科。

考核方式 Assessment

  • Attendance: 10%
  • Assignments: 25%
